# Recap

The user was away and is back. Reply with a recap of this conversation and
nothing else:

- Under 40 words, one or two plain sentences, no markdown.
- Start with the overall goal and what's being worked on now, then the one
  next step.
- Leave out how a problem was found, how a fix works inside, smaller to-dos
  and side remarks.
- Don't call any tools, don't carry on with the work and don't ask anything.
